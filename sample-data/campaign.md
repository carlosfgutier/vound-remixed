# Sample campaign — "State of AI in GTM" roundtable

Paste these into the form at `/campaigns/new` and upload `contacts.csv` below.

## Campaign type

> Follow-up to senior operators across EMEA and LATAM who downloaded our "State of AI in GTM 2026" report in the last 30 days. We want to invite 20 of them to a closed-door virtual roundtable where we'll share unreleased benchmark data and hear how they're actually deploying AI across their revenue teams. The ask is a 45-minute session, Chatham House rules, no sales pitch.

## Audience

> 10 execs and senior operators across EMEA (Denmark, Sweden, Germany, Portugal) and LATAM (Brazil), plus one MIT contact. All downloaded the gated report from our site — the form only required email, so for most people we have nothing beyond the address and the domain it came from. Mix of coffee/retail (Espresso House, Flying Tiger), Nordic SaaS (Visma, Risma), consumer DTC (Emma Sleep), IT services (NeoTalent), research (Wilke, MIT), and a Google Brazil contact. We need enrichment to figure out who's actually senior enough to invite and whether their role is close enough to RevOps / GTM / marketing ops for the roundtable to land.

## Ranked goals

1. **Book a sales call / intro meeting** — the roundtable invite is the wedge; the real goal is pipeline.
2. **Qualify the lead (ICP fit, budget, timeline)** — we don't want to waste 20 seats on people who aren't buyers.

## Contacts

See `contacts.csv` in this folder — 10 rows, email + best-effort name/company parsed from the address. Drag that file into the CSV upload zone.
